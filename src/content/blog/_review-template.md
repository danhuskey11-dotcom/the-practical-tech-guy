Start with a short summary of what the product is and the most important conclusion from the review.

Avoid making the reader wait until the bottom to understand your overall impression.

## Quick Verdict

Summarize the recommendation in a few sentences.

**Best for:** Describe the ideal user.

**Skip it if:** Describe the situation where another option would make more sense.

## What I Reviewed

Explain exactly what product, version, plan, or hardware configuration was evaluated.

Include relevant details such as:

- Product or service name
- Version or model
- Subscription tier
- Hardware configuration
- Operating system
- Testing period
- Purchase price or subscription price when relevant

## What I Like

### Strength One

Explain the benefit and why it matters in practical use.

### Strength Two

Explain another meaningful advantage.

### Strength Three

Explain another meaningful advantage.

## What I Don't Like

### Weakness One

Explain the limitation and who is likely to care about it.

### Weakness Two

Explain another trade-off.

Don't hide weaknesses simply because the product has an affiliate program.

## Setup and Ease of Use

Explain what installation, configuration, onboarding, or initial setup is actually like.

Mention any unexpected requirements or difficulties.

## Features That Actually Matter

Focus on the features that affect real-world use instead of repeating the manufacturer's feature list.

## Performance and Reliability

Describe relevant observations about:

- Speed
- Stability
- Resource use
- Reliability
- Compatibility
- Battery impact
- Network performance

Only include areas that are relevant to the product being reviewed.

## Pricing and Value

Explain the pricing structure and what the buyer actually receives.

Compare the price with realistic alternatives where appropriate.

Do not evaluate value based only on the affiliate commission.

## Who Should Buy It?

Describe the users or situations where the product makes sense.

## Who Should Skip It?

Explain when another product, service, or approach would be better.

## Alternatives to Consider

List meaningful alternatives and briefly explain why someone might choose them instead.

## Final Verdict

Summarize:

- What the product does well
- Its most important drawback
- Who should consider it
- Whether you believe it offers good value

Keep the conclusion practical and specific.
